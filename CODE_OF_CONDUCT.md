# Code of Conduct

AlgoViz is a learning tool, and many people here are beginners. Everyone should feel welcome to ask a question, report a problem or send a first pull request.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience and different ways of learning.
- Say thank you, and credit other people's work.

Don't:

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Make sexual, violent or discriminatory comments or images.
- Publish someone's private information without their permission.
- Mock someone for not knowing something.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@somyacodes07](https://github.com/somyacodes07), privately through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who repeatedly does so.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
